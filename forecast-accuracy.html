ARR NODE · USE CASE

Your forecast is built on rep happy ears — not what's actually in the deal.

The ARR Node reads every transcript, email, and note on active deals every night — then scores each deal against your qualification methodology and writes objective readouts back to Salesforce or HubSpot. Inside your own AWS account. Forecast within 1% of the called number.

The problem

Your forecast accuracy problem isn't effort — it's subjectivity. Deal inspection eats leadership bandwidth every week, and the number you call is still a guess dressed up as a methodology. Someone has to inspect every deal to know whether the forecast is real or a rep's happy ears — and at most companies, that someone is the most expensive person on the team.

"We were drowning in human subjectivity. Our forecast and pipeline health were basically built on rep happy ears and gut feelings." — Alex Burkholder, VP Revenue, AskNicely

The result is predictable: slipped quarters, board surprises, and deal reviews that surface risk too late to act on. Humans review deals weekly at best, and only the deals that look important. The coverage gap is structural — not a hiring problem.

How the ARR Node works

Deployed inside your own AWS account — one Terraform command, 29 minutes to stand up infrastructure. Your CRM connects via webhooks; results write back nightly (or on every inbound event for pipeline).

ARR NODE
  • Reads every transcript, email, and note on active deals — every night.
  • Scores deals against your qualification methodology, not a generic framework.
  • Surfaces risk without bias to prevent slipped quarters.
  • Flags the gap between what the rep says in the CRM and what the transcript actually shows.
  • Writes objective readouts back to Salesforce or HubSpot — data never leaves your VPC.
