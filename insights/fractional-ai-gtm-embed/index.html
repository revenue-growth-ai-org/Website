Who this is for

This is for the VP or Director of Revenue Operations at a Series A–C B2B SaaS company doing roughly $5M–$50M ARR. Salesforce or HubSpot is the system of record. Forecast and pipeline numbers are arguments, not facts. Leadership wants “AI in GTM” and you own the blast radius if it fails.

Sometimes the Engineering Lead sits in the room too — the person who can read a Terraform plan and cares where data lives. When the conversation turns to a control plane in your own AWS account, that person matters. Until then, RevOps owns the problem.

You’re not looking for another Zapier cookbook. You’re choosing between DIY agent builds, a full-time hire, or something that gets you cleanup and process this quarter.

What six months buys

Six-month AI GTM / RevOps embed — cleanup, process, then agents. Platform included when it fits.

That’s the default line. It means a sequence, not a catalog of tools.

Cleanup

Fields that don’t mean anything. Stages that reps ignore. Ownership that jumps between AE, SDR, and CS with no handoff. Reports that require a human interpreter.

Cleanup is boring. It’s also why every agent project dies. You can’t automate a pipeline definition that three people disagree on.

Process

Once the data can hold a truth, you fix how work moves. What “qualified” means. Who owns forecast. When a deal advances. What gets logged versus what gets guessed.

Process is the difference between a dashboard and an operating system. Agents amplify whatever you have. Garbage process becomes garbage at scale.

Agents

Only then. Agents on a clean foundation can score, route, nudge, summarize, and surface risk without inventing reality. Agents on a messy CRM invent fiction faster than your team can catch it.

Proof, not a pitch deck

Same CRM. No magic model. Cleanup and process first. Agents after. That’s the war story. The rest is noise.

What is a fractional AI GTM engineer?

An engineer embedded part-time in your revenue team who cleans the CRM, fixes the sales process, and then builds AI agents in your stack. Revenue-Growth.ai does this as a six-month AI GTM / RevOps embed.

What order does the work happen in?

Cleanup, then process, then agents. Agents built on dirty CRM data automate the mess. The order does not change.

How long does a fractional GTM engagement last?

Revenue-Growth.ai runs a six-month embed. Agents ship on a two-week working rhythm once the data and process are ready, and your team owns everything at handoff.

Do I need to clean up my CRM before deploying AI agents?

Yes. An agent reads whatever is in the CRM. Duplicate accounts, missing owners, and loose stage definitions become wrong drafts at scale. Cleanup comes first.

Mostly emphasis. RevOps engineers own the CRM and process; GTM engineers own the automation on top. The embed covers both, in that order.
