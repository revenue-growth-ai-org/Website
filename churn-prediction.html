GRR/NRR NODE · USE CASE

You find out about churn at renewal — when it's too late to save.

The GRR/NRR Node replaces subjective health scores with an AI maturity model built from actual customer interactions — scored weekly, flagging churn risk months before the renewal conversation and surfacing expansion opportunities your team can't see.

The problem

You find out about churn at the renewal conversation, when it's too late to save. The CS team scores every account, but the score has zero correlation with who actually leaves at renewal. Someone has to review every account to know which customers are quietly walking toward the exit — and almost nobody has bandwidth to do it systematically.

At most companies, customer health is a subjective exercise: a CSM's gut feel dressed up as a score. Expansion opportunity sits inside your existing base right now — there's just no systematic way to find it before a competitor does.

Your next best deal is already a customer — you just can't see it. The pattern behind the best retention quarters isn't magic — it's coverage: agents review every account, every night, with no bias about which logos matter.

How the GRR/NRR Node works

Deployed inside your own AWS account — one Terraform command, 29 minutes to stand up infrastructure. Your CRM connects via webhooks; results write back nightly (or on every inbound event for pipeline).

GRR/NRR NODE
  • Replaces subjective health scores with an AI maturity model built from actual interactions.
  • Automatically flags churn risk before renewal — months ahead, not weeks.
  • Surfaces hidden expansion opportunities from actual customer conversations.
  • Scores every account by maturity, renewal timing, and size of prize weekly.
  • Writes objective health and expansion readouts back to your CRM inside your VPC.
