PIPELINE NODE · USE CASE

Inbound leads wait while reps do hours of manual pre-call research.

The Pipeline Node instantly evaluates inbound intent, researches the account, and routes to the right rep with a synthesized brief — turning hours of manual pre-call research into seconds. Deployed inside your Salesforce or HubSpot stack, running in your own AWS account.

The problem

Every inbound lead triggers the same hidden tax: someone has to evaluate intent, research the account, and decide which rep should own it — before the first conversation happens. At most B2B SaaS companies, that work is manual, slow, and inconsistent. Response time suffers while reps dig through LinkedIn, news, and CRM history.

Static workflow tools can route by territory or round-robin, but they can't read context and apply judgment. They don't synthesize a brief. They don't know that this "small" inbound logo is actually a strategic expansion target — or that this enterprise inquiry should bypass the SDR queue entirely.

The Pipeline Node applies the same autonomous judgment a sharp RevOps analyst would — except it runs on every inbound event, instantly, with your customer data never leaving your VPC.

How the Pipeline Node works

Deployed inside your own AWS account — one Terraform command, 29 minutes to stand up infrastructure. Your CRM connects via webhooks; results write back nightly (or on every inbound event for pipeline).

PIPELINE NODE
  • Instantly evaluates inbound intent and researches the account on every webhook event.
  • Routes to the right rep with a synthesized brief — not just a territory rule.
  • Turns hours of manual pre-call research into seconds of automated prep.
  • Connects to Salesforce or HubSpot via webhooks through an ALB in your VPC.
  • Hybrid routing: static rules for volume, Claude for ambiguous edge cases.
